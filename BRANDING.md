# Borea Branding

**Borea** and the **Borea logo** identify the official Borea project.

The Borea source code is open source and available under the MIT License. The project name and logo are separate from the software license.

## Forks and modified versions

Borea is open source and forks are welcome.

You may create, modify, and distribute your own versions of Borea. When doing so, please use your own name and branding so that your version can be clearly distinguished from the official Borea project.

You may accurately describe your project as:

- "based on Borea"
- "a fork of Borea"
- "a modified version of Borea"
- "compatible with Borea"

provided that the description does not suggest that your project is the official Borea project or is endorsed by its maintainers.

## Name

Please do not use the name **Borea** as the primary name of an unofficial distribution, application, or service in a way that could reasonably cause users to believe that it is the official Borea project.

Truthful references to Borea are welcome, including references in documentation, compatibility information, comparisons, and descriptions of derivative projects.

## Logo

The Borea logo is reserved for identifying the official Borea project.

Please do not use the Borea logo as the primary branding, application icon, or identity of an unofficial version in a way that could cause confusion about its origin or affiliation.

You may use the logo when truthfully referring to the official Borea project, such as in documentation or links to the official project.

## Official project

The official Borea project is represented by the project's official repositories and communication channels.

If you are unsure whether a project, release, download, or communication is official, refer to the links published by the Borea project itself.

## Questions

If you have a legitimate use of the Borea name or logo that is not covered here, please contact the Borea maintainers.
